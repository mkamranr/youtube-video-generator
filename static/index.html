Jobs

Videos for your YouTube channel — generated, reviewed, published.

New Video

Paste YouTube URLs (one per line). Each becomes a job: transcript → LLM script → soundtrack → AI clips synced to the audio → final video.

Content
You have no channel yet — create one in Settings → Channels first.
Sound & format
Your own music
Optional. Upload a track and it becomes the soundtrack as-is — no song is generated, and it, not the Video length, decides how long the video is (10 seconds to 6 minutes).
Story video
The LLM invents the scenes that build the story, and the voiceover is only what happens and what the characters say — never a description of what is on screen. You review and edit every line before anything is rendered.
Song lyrics
Leave this empty and the LLM writes the song. Paste lyrics and they are sung exactly as written — and they, not the Video length, decide how long the video is.
On-screen story board
Styled fact cards (key facts, numbers, takeaways) with related images are drawn over the video, timed to what is being said. Each card uses one of eight layouts, picked from its own content, so no two cards in a row look the same. On by default for Tech channels.
Word-synced subtitles over the video, with the spoken word highlighted. The words come from the script itself, so they are never a mis-hearing. On by default for Tech channels.
The opening line shown full-screen over the first seconds, a subscribe card at the end, a thin progress bar and a short whoosh as each card appears. On by default for Tech channels.
Page scroll overlay
A screenshot of the repository / model page scrolls slowly as a large panel over the middle of the video; the opening and the ending stay clear. If the page cannot be captured, the video is still made from the README.
Art style
Sets the look of every shot and the thumbnail.
